# Taste Skill v2: Anti-Slop Frontend Art Direction

> A page built by an AI model usually looks like the average of every page the model has seen: a centered headline over three lines, a little pill label above it, a row of identical icon cards, a soft green/purple gradient, and raw emojis scattered everywhere. Nothing in it is wrong, but nothing in it was decided.

This skill replaces those defaults with **deliberate decisions grounded in the subject itself**.

---

## 1. Subject-Grounded Design

1. **The idea comes from the subject, not styling trends.**
   - For **BeejSetu (बीजसेतु — "The Seed Bridge")**: The design stems from seed biology, sun-drying on unbleached cloth, seasonal monsoon cycles, rich Western Ghats soil, and the tactile reality of kitchen fruit seed saving.
   - Contrast between *dormant potential in a dry seed* and *expansive living canopy* reflected in deep forest green (`#0E2A1D`), terracotta clay (`#A44A29`), sun-dried seed ochre (`#D49938`), and warm unbleached khadi paper (`#F8F5EE`).

2. **The Competitor Test:**
   - If you can swap in a generic e-commerce plant nursery or commercial seed seller and the page still makes sense, the design is too generic.
   - Anchor the page directly to its real identity: non-commercial, trust-first, indigenous seed exchange piloting in Pune & Western Ghats, connecting household fruit savers with recognized grassroots afforestation NGOs (WePlant, Navdanya, Annadana, Sahaja Samrudha).

---

## 2. Editorial Headline & Copy Discipline

1. **Every headline fits in 2 lines or fewer at every screen width.**
   - Target ~32–45 characters for the primary hero title.
   - No runaway 5-line sentences pretending to be headings.
2. **The first screen answers the visitor's first question immediately:**
   - *What is this?* A bridge connecting domestic fruit & vegetable seed savers with verified planting missions.
   - *Is it for me?* Yes, whether you have 10 Jamun seeds from breakfast or want free native seeds for your terrace.
   - *What do I do?* Start the 4-step donation wizard or order free indigenous seeds.
3. **One primary action on the first screen:**
   - Unambiguous button label (`Donate Seeds`).
   - Accompanied by a calm secondary link (`Order Native Seeds` / `How It Works`).

---

## 3. The Anti-Slop House Style

- **No Pill Badges as Decoration:** Don't put a glowing rounded badge saying "✨ The #1 Seed Network" above the headline with a blinking neon dot.
- **No Floating Glassmorphism Cards:** Don't add fake floating cards with random stats or generic fruit emojis hovering over imagery.
- **Whole Fields of Color:** Use full sections of calm, rich tones (e.g. warm khadi paper canvas `#F8F5EE`, deep forest contrast blocks `#0E2A1D`) rather than scattered confetti colors.
- **One "Live Thing":** Give the visitor interactive, tangible preview tools:
  1. The **Interactive Germination Viability Test Calculator** (calculating real sprout percentages and ecological advice).
  2. The **4-Step Donation Wizard** (step navigation with dynamic Green Seed Champion certificate generation).

---

## 4. Verification Check Before Emitting UI

- Does this look like an award-winning botanical journal or a generic startup template?
- Are all icons clean, monochrome SVGs with identical stroke weights?
- Are interactive elements keyboard accessible with visible focus rings?
- Is there zero placeholder text and zero fabricated statistics?
- Are all existing interactive capabilities (cart, donation wizard, orders, activity drawer) 100% functional?
