# Maharashtra Indigenous Seed Conservation & Partner NGO Research

**Project:** BeejSetu (बीजसेतु) — Indigenous Seed Bridge & Community Agroforestry Network  
**Pilot Geography:** Pune, Western Ghats & Maharashtra Eco-Belt  
**Document Purpose:** Verification and field-grounding of Maharashtra-based NGOs, community seed banks, and ecological foundations that accept seed donations, collect native fruit/tree seeds, and conserve indigenous crop landraces.

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Geographic Rationale

BeejSetu is tailored as a regional conservation bridge operating in the Pune and Western Ghats eco-belt. Sourcing and directing native seeds across vast interstate distances causes logistical friction, reduced seed viability during transit, and ecological mismatch (e.g. planting non-native ecotypes in Western Ghats soils).

By partnering exclusively with **verified Maharashtra-based organizations**, BeejSetu ensures:
1. **Ecological Coherence:** Native seed varieties (such as Jamun *Syzygium cumini*, Desi Mango *Mangifera indica*, Sitaphal *Annona squamosa*, Amla *Phyllanthus emblica*) are propagated within their native Western Ghats and Deccan Plateau bio-climatic zones.
2. **Accessible Physical Drop-Off & Dispatch:** Donors across Pune, Satara, Ahmednagar, Thane, Mumbai, and surrounding districts can directly drop off or dispatch dried seed parcels to nearby regional collection centers.
3. **Deep Grassroots Pedigree:** Working with organizations spearheaded by renowned conservationists, national awardees, and pioneer institutions ensures every donated seed is nurtured in community nurseries, agroforestry wadis, and sacred grove (*Devrai*) restorations.

## 4. Preservation & Implementation Checkpoints

To adhere strictly to the **"Do No Harm"** standard:
1. **DOM IDs & Handlers:** Container `#ngo-cards-grid`, prefill handler `prefillDonationForNgo()`, and step transitions remain unmodified.
2. **Object Structure:** `NGO_PARTNERS` entries maintain keys (`id`, `name`, `tagline`, `primaryRole`, `address`, `city`, `state`, `phone`, `altPhone`, `email`, `website`, `acceptedSeeds`, `donationsReceived`, `rating`, `verified`).
3. **Data Linkage:** In `js/seed-data.js`, the `acceptedBy` arrays in `SEED_DATABASE` are updated to match these Maharashtra entities cleanly.
4. **Local State & Fallback:** In `js/app.js`, the fallback string for `targetNgo` defaults to `"Sahyadri Devrai / Maharashtra Partner Network"`.
5. **UI Text Integrity:** Feature bullets, wizard step descriptions, and footer partner link references updated from non-Maharashtra organizations to the newly verified Maharashtra entities.
