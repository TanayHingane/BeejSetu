How BeejSetu Works

Two Paths, One Living Canopy

Whether you're saving seeds from your kitchen counter or planting an orchard, BeejSetu connects you directly to fertile soil.

For Donors & Urban Citizens

I Want to Donate Seeds

Saved seeds from mangoes, jamuns, sitaphal, or organic vegetables? Don't discard them into landfills. Wash and dry them; we collect and route them to recognized agroforestry missions for large-scale afforestation.

  • Free doorstep pickup or drop-off at nearest verified partner NGO
  • Supported by Sahyadri Devrai, BAIF Pune, Lokpanchayat & Vanarai
  • Dynamic digital Green Seed Champion Certificate of Stewardship
For Forests & Farmers

Where Do Donated Seeds Go?

We do not sell seeds. Every seed you donate is handed directly to verified Maharashtra NGOs (like Sahyadri Devrai and BAIF). Nurseries raise them into saplings for afforestation, while hardy seeds are donated directly to smallholder farmers.

  • Nurtured into saplings in community Vruksha Banks for Western Ghats revival
  • Distributed to marginal farmers in drought-prone belts for agroforestry
  • 100% non-commercial citizen-to-soil ecological stewardship

Seed Saving 101

How to Prepare Seeds from Your Kitchen

Don't let viable seeds rot. Follow this foolproof 4-step preparation protocol used by botanical seed banks.

01

Source Native Produce

Pick fully ripened desi, organic, or local fruits and veggies. Avoid imported hybrid grocery produce whose seeds are often sterile or weak.

02

Wash & Strip Pulp

Scrub away fruit sugars under cold water. For tomatoes and melons, soak in a jar of water for 48 hours to dissolve the sprouting-inhibiting gel.

03

Sun Dry for 48 Hours

Spread across cotton cloths or wooden boards. Sun-dry until moisture drops below 8%. Never oven-bake or microwave seeds!

04

Store in Paper Bags

Place seeds in breathable brown paper envelopes with a pinch of dry wood ash or neem powder to deter pests and moisture.
